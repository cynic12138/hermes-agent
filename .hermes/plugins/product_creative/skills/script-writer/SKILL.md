# Script Writer

Expand one approved direction into a complete story. Every beat must perform a
narrative function and remain grounded in confirmed product and compliance evidence.

## When to Use

Use only after a Creative Decision selects a candidate and before shot planning,
Provider compilation, or production.

## Inputs

Read the selected candidate, Creative Decision, Product Grounding, user constraints,
research references, target duration, channel, and selected materials.

## Procedure

1. Define a premise, setting, world rules, and motivated characters.
2. Create a specific visual and audio hook.
3. Establish an inciting incident and a conflict that can escalate on screen.
4. Make the product intervention necessary to the story without inventing efficacy.
5. Resolve the conflict and end on a restrained, memorable brand beat.
6. Label the narrative function of every beat.

## Output Contract

Produce `product_creative.story_package.v1` with premise, characters, setting,
world rules, hooks, incident, conflict, escalation, turn, product intervention,
ending, dialogue, prohibited content, and narrative functions.

## Tool Boundary

Read approved artifacts and materials. Do not change the selected direction, search
new sources, create shots, call Providers, or update Product Brain.

## Failure Conditions

Fail when the direction is unapproved, conflict is absent, the product is decorative,
the story depends on an unconfirmed claim, or the ending does not resolve the setup.

## Quality Rubric

- The first beat is visually specific.
- Character action follows motivation.
- Conflict escalates rather than merely listing information.
- Product entry causes a legitimate turn.
- Ending pays off the hook and fits the channel duration.

## Positive Examples

Objects competing for one bag position creates a spatial conflict; the fixed product
plate changes the choice and resolves the action without claiming treatment effects.

## Negative Examples

Do not write “show product, show details, add benefits, end with logo.” That is a
display sequence, not a story.

## Verification

Trace the premise through hook, incident, escalation, turn, product intervention,
and ending; confirm every claim is allowed.
