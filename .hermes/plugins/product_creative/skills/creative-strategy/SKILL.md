# Creative Strategy

Create three production-aware directions without calling a generation Provider.
Product truth stays fixed while scene, mechanism, story, emotion, and style may vary.

## When to Use

Use after Product Grounding and Research are ready and before scriptwriting,
storyboarding, production, or user preview.

## Inputs

Read the Creative Task Brief, Product Grounding Pack, Research Insight Pack,
selected materials, confirmed preferences, and comparable historical artifacts.

## Procedure

1. Identify the product's necessary role and the viewer's reason to stop.
2. Build `stable` from proven, low-risk structures.
3. Build `variation` by changing a meaningful narrative or visual mechanism.
4. Build `exploration` with a new world rule, genre, or presentation device.
5. Give every direction a hook, conflict, progression, turn, ending, production
   route, source refs, risks, and historical difference.
6. Reject candidates that differ only in adjectives, captions, or camera decoration.

## Output Contract

Produce exactly three `product_creative.creative_candidate.v1` artifacts with one
candidate per direction.

## Tool Boundary

Read artifacts, history, and materials. Do not search live sources, mutate Product
Brain, create Provider payloads, or generate media.

## Failure Conditions

Fail when grounding is insufficient, research is unavailable without an accepted
degradation, candidates are not distinct, the product role is decorative, or the
history difference is unproven.

## Quality Rubric

- Specific 0–5 second stop reason.
- Complete conflict, progression, turn, and ending.
- Product is necessary but not used for an unconfirmed claim.
- Directions are structurally different and feasible.
- Every borrowed pattern has a source and adaptation rule.

## Positive Examples

Stable uses a real-life time conflict; variation uses object comedy; exploration
uses a future-message rule. All three preserve the same confirmed packaging.

## Negative Examples

“温暖版、清新版、高级版” with the same opening, sequence, and ending is one idea,
not three candidates.

## Verification

Compare hook, conflict, product role, mechanism, ending, source refs, and historical
similarity across all three candidates before accepting them.
